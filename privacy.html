ZAKKIRNI Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026
App: ZAKKIRNI (Android, package name: com.izzettin.zikir)
Developer: Ahmet Genco
Contact: ahmetgenco2002@gmail.com

ZAKKIRNI is an Islamic app for daily adhkar (remembrances), Quran wird tracking, prayer times and alerts, and a friendly points and ranking system. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Display name and an optional nickname (kunya), for example a family name. These are shown to other users in rankings, leagues, circles and follow lists.
  • Country and a chosen avatar.
  • Messages you send through the "Contact us" form (your message and the optional name you type).
  • Reports you send about another user (only the reported user's public ID and a short reason).

Information the app creates

  • Progress data: points, daily streaks, achievements/badges, adhkar and wird completion logs, and settings. This is stored on your device.
  • Random account ID and public ID: a random identifier created by the app (not linked to your name, phone or email) and a public number other users can use to find or follow you.
  • Ranking data sent to our server: display name, optional nickname, country, total and monthly points, streak, and weekly league points. If you enable "anonymous ranking", your name, nickname and country are not shown to others.
  • Backup: a copy of your progress data is stored on our server so you can restore it with your recovery code on another phone. Keep your code private: anyone who has it can restore your data.
  • Circles: if you create or join a circle (family/friends group), the circle name, member names, roles, reserved Quran parts and completion status are stored and shown to the circle's members.
  • Crash reports (optional, can be turned off in Settings): technical error details such as app version, platform, language and the error message. They do not contain your name or personal data.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device location (while the app is in use) only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. You can also choose a city manually instead. Your location is saved on your device. To get prayer times, the app sends the coordinates (or the city and country) to a prayer-times service (see Section 4).

Advertising and purchases

  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, device information and approximate location to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Optional support payments and the optional "Remove ads" subscription are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or payment details; we only learn whether a purchase or subscription is active.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features: adhkar reminders, prayer times and alerts, points, streaks, achievements, rankings, leagues and circles.
  • To let you restore your progress on a new phone.
  • To show other users your public profile (name, optional nickname, country, points, streak) in rankings and lists, unless you choose anonymous ranking.
  • To respond to your messages, review reports, and keep the app safe (for example, hiding names or users reported as inappropriate).
  • To find and fix errors (crash reports).
  • To show ads and process purchases and subscriptions.

3. Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device (adhkar, prayer reminders, Quran circle reminders). They are created on your phone; no personal data is sent to create them. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your phone settings.

4. Services we use (third parties)

  • Supabase (database and backend hosting): stores ranking data, backups, circles, reports and crash reports.
  • Aladhan API (api.aladhan.com): receives location coordinates or city/country to return prayer times.
  • Formspree: delivers messages sent through the "Contact us" form to the developer's email.
  • Google AdMob: advertising.
  • Google Play services / Billing: app delivery, support payments and subscriptions.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the services above, as needed to run the app, or when required by law.

5. What other users can see

Other users can see your display name, optional nickname, country, avatar (in your circles), points, streak and league position in rankings, leagues, follow lists and circles. They cannot see your location, your recovery code or your adhkar log. Choose a display name that does not reveal more than you want to share.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you delete the user inside the app or uninstall the app.
  • Deleting your user inside the app removes your ranking entry and backup from our server on the next connection.
  • You can also ask us to delete your server data by emailing ahmetgenco2002@gmail.com with your public ID (shown in the app). We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after about 30 days.

7. Children

ZAKKIRNI is a general-audience religious app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data sent between the app and our server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your recovery code private.

9. Your choices

  • Use anonymous ranking, or do not enter a nickname.
  • Turn crash reports and notifications off in Settings.
  • Deny or revoke location permission and choose your city manually.
  • Reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings, or subscribe to remove ads.
  • Delete your user in the app, or contact us to delete your server data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, and the effective date above will change.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: ahmetgenco2002@gmail.com

ZAKKIRNI; günlük zikirler, Kur'an virdi takibi, namaz vakitleri ve bildirimleri ile dostane bir puan ve sıralama sistemi sunan İslami bir uygulamadır. Bu politika, uygulamanın hangi bilgileri neden kullandığını ve sahip olduğun seçenekleri açıklar. Uygulamayı kullanarak bu politikayı kabul etmiş olursun.

1. Topladığımız bilgiler

Bize verdiğin bilgiler

  • Görünen ad ve isteğe bağlı bir takma ad (künye), örneğin soyadı. Bunlar sıralamalarda, liglerde, halkalarda ve takip listelerinde diğer kullanıcılara gösterilir.
  • Ülke ve seçtiğin avatar.
  • "Bize ulaş" formundan gönderdiğin mesajlar (mesajın ve isteğe bağlı yazdığın ad).
  • Başka bir kullanıcı hakkında gönderdiğin bildirimler (yalnızca bildirilen kullanıcının herkese açık kimliği ve kısa bir sebep).

Uygulamanın oluşturduğu bilgiler

  • İlerleme verileri: puanlar, günlük seriler, başarılar/rozetler, zikir ve vird tamamlama kayıtları ile ayarlar. Bunlar cihazında saklanır.
  • Rastgele hesap kimliği ve herkese açık kimlik: uygulamanın oluşturduğu rastgele bir tanımlayıcı (adınla, telefonunla veya e-postanla ilişkili değildir) ve diğer kullanıcıların seni bulmak ya da takip etmek için kullanabileceği herkese açık bir numara.
  • Sunucumuza gönderilen sıralama verileri: görünen ad, isteğe bağlı takma ad, ülke, toplam ve aylık puan, seri ve haftalık lig puanı. "Anonim sıralama"yı açarsan adın, takma adın ve ülken başkalarına gösterilmez.
  • Yedek: ilerleme verilerinin bir kopyası sunucumuzda saklanır; böylece başka bir telefonda kurtarma kodunla geri yükleyebilirsin. Kodunu gizli tut: kodu bilen herkes verilerini geri yükleyebilir.
  • Halkalar: bir halka (aile/arkadaş grubu) oluşturursan veya katılırsan; halka adı, üye adları, roller, ayrılan Kur'an cüzleri ve tamamlanma durumu saklanır ve halka üyelerine gösterilir.
  • Hata raporları (isteğe bağlı, Ayarlar'dan kapatılabilir): uygulama sürümü, platform, dil ve hata mesajı gibi teknik ayrıntılar. Adını veya kişisel verilerini içermez.

Konum

İzin verirsen uygulama, cihaz konumunu (yalnızca uygulama kullanılırken) sadece namaz vakitlerini ve kıble yönünü hesaplamak için kullanır. İstersen şehri elle de seçebilirsin. Konumun cihazında saklanır. Namaz vakitlerini almak için uygulama, koordinatları (veya şehir ve ülkeyi) bir namaz vakti hizmetine gönderir (bkz. Bölüm 4).
